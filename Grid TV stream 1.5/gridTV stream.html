GridTV STREAM Help

Everything you need to know to get up and running. GridTV STREAM is a browser-based TV interface for live channels, secondary streams, recordings, custom apps, and M3U playlists — all running entirely in your browser with local storage.

Getting started
Dismissing the screensaver
After three hours of inactivity, a screensaver appears with scenic photos, a bouncing banner, and a clock. The banner says "Press Space to watch TV."
To dismiss the screensaver and open the Home menu, press the Space bar on your keyboard. No other key will dismiss it.
The Home menu
Everything lives in the left sidebar. Click a tab to switch to it:
  • Dashboard — quick access to live channels, recent recordings, stream picks, and daily recommendations
  • Live TV — the channel guide and program grid
  • Stream — a timeline-style view of your stream playlists
  • Recordings — everything you've recorded with the DVR
  • Apps — custom HTML apps you've uploaded
  • Packages — where you load and manage playlists
  • Search — look up any channel by name
  • Help — this guide
Managing M3U playlists
Two kinds of playlists
GridTV STREAM handles two separate playlist categories:
  • Live TV playlists — your main channel list. These appear in Live TV, the program grid, Search, and the Dashboard.
  • Stream playlists — a secondary list shown only in the Stream timeline and Stream Picks on the Dashboard. Useful for anything you want kept apart from your main channels.
Loading a playlist
1
Open Packages in the sidebar.
2
Click Upload playlist for Live TV, or Upload to Stream for the Stream tab.
3
Select one or more .m3u or .m3u8 files.
You can also upload a Live TV playlist from inside the guide itself — click Upload playlist in the top-right of the Live TV view.
Removing a playlist
In the Packages tab, find the playlist in the Live TV Playlists or Stream Playlists section and click the × on its row. All channels from that playlist are removed.
What's inside an M3U file
Each entry has metadata followed by a stream URL. GridTV STREAM reads:
  • tvg-logo — the channel's logo image
  • group-title — the channel's category
  • tvg-chno — an explicit channel number (optional)
  • The URL on the next line — the stream source
Channels without a URL are skipped.
Watching live TV & changing channels
Watch a channel
From Live TV → Channels, click any channel card in the list on the left. The video starts on the right and the active channel glows.
Play / Pause / Mute
Use the buttons under the video. Space also toggles play/pause while you're watching — but not while typing in a text input.
Fullscreen
Click anywhere on the video to go fullscreen. Press Escape, or click Exit Fullscreen in the corner, to come back.
The program grid
From Live TV → Guide. The left column lists your channels; the right shows colored program bars. Click a channel or bar to see its details in the bottom strip, then click Watch to switch to it. The bars are decorative — they don't reflect real schedules.
Searching channels
Open Search in the sidebar and type a channel name. Results appear live — click one to jump straight to that channel. The on-screen keyboard pops up automatically when you click the search box.
The Stream tab
What it is
The Stream tab shows your Stream playlists in a timeline guide. Each row is one stream channel, and the colored bars show a synthetic "program" layout that changes daily.
Using it
  • Click a row or a bar to select a stream — its details show at the bottom.
  • Click ▶ Watch to play it in the main player.
  • The Stream tab is separate from Live TV — playing a stream doesn't affect your live channel selection.
Recording with DVR
Start recording
Two ways, both for live channels only:
  • Click the small red circle on the right side of any channel card
  • Click ● REC under the video for the channel you're watching
While recording, the button pulses red, the time readout turns red, and the status badges show ● REC.
Stop recording
Click the same button again — it now reads ■ STOP or is highlighted green if the channel was already recorded. The recording is finalized and saved to local storage.
Watch a recording
Open Recordings in the sidebar and click any finished recording to play it back. Recordings also appear in Jump Back In on the Dashboard.
Delete a recording
Click the × on any recording in the Recordings tab. If it's still recording, this stops it first.
CORS and DRM caveat
Some streams block browser-based recording. If you see "no data captured," the stream itself prevented it — no amount of retrying will fix that for a protected stream.
Apps
What Apps are
Any HTML file you upload in the Apps tab becomes a custom app in GridTV STREAM. It shows up as a card in the Apps grid with its auto-extracted icon and name.
Loading and launching apps
Click Upload App, pick an .html file. Click the resulting card to launch the app in a sandboxed full-screen viewer. Click Close App in the top bar to return to the dashboard.
Deleting apps
Hover a card and click the red × in its top-right corner to remove the app.
The Dashboard
What's on it
  • Channel rail at the top — your most-watched channels, ordered by usage. Click a pill to load it into the hero preview.
  • Hero preview — a live preview of the selected channel with a big Watch Now button.
  • Jump Back In — your recent recordings. Click one to play it.
  • Stream Picks — a grid of your most-watched streams. Click a tile to play it.
  • Recommended Today — a daily rotation of channels, seeded by the current date (it's the same list all day and changes tomorrow).
On-screen keyboard
Typing without a keyboard
When you click into a text box (like Search), an on-screen keyboard pops up. Click keys to type, switch layers with ABC / 123 / #+=, drag the keyboard by its title bar, or close it with the × in its corner.
What saves automatically
Your data persists
These all save automatically in your browser:
  • Live TV playlists and Stream playlists
  • DVR recordings (metadata + video blobs)
  • Uploaded apps
  • Watch counts (used for the Dashboard's most-watched ordering)
  • Where you dragged the on-screen keyboard
Clearing browser data wipes everything
Warning: If you clear your browser's data for GridTV STREAM, you'll lose all your playlists, recordings, apps, and watch history. There's no cloud backup.
Going offline
Automatic offline detection
GridTV STREAM checks your connection every few seconds. If it fails, a black screen appears saying the channel is unavailable, and all videos pause. When you reconnect, everything comes back automatically.
